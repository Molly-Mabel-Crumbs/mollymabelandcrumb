Your 2-day timeline

Day 1 — Morning

Feed Mabel

Discard down to your seed amount, then add flour and water per the amounts above. Leave at room temperature.

Day 1 — 4 to 6 hours later

Check Mabel — is she at peak?

She should have doubled (or more) and be domed at the top. Do the float test: drop a small spoonful in water — if it floats, she's ready. If not, give her more time.

Day 1 — When Mabel peaks

Mix your dough

Combine your flour, water, starter, and salt. Autolyse for 30 minutes then begin stretch and folds.

Day 1 — Afternoon / Evening

Bulk fermentation + shape

4–6 hours of bulk ferment at room temp (4 sets of stretch & folds in the first 2 hours). Shape your loaves, place in bannetons, cover.

Day 1 — Night

Into the fridge

Cold proof overnight — 8 to 16 hours. This develops flavour and makes scoring easier. Don't skip this.

Day 2 — Morning

Preheat, score & bake 🍞

Preheat oven + dutch oven to 475°F for 45–60 min. Score straight from the fridge, bake lid on 18–20 min, remove lid, drop to 450°F and bake another 18–20 min until internal temp hits 205–210°F.

Mabel tip — always keep some back Never use all of your starter in a bake. Always keep at least 20–30g back so you can feed her again for the next bake. The amounts above already account for this — the "use in dough" amount leaves Mabel with enough to carry on.
